## 3. The Definitive Weekly Routine
To avoid domain availability conflicts, structure your week precisely using this scheduled cycle:
```
[Monday / Thursday]  --> Focus entirely on Diona (Mondstadt Domain)
[Tuesday / Friday]   --> Focus entirely on Normal Overworld Bosses
[Wednesday / Sat]    --> Heavy Hitters (Choose freely between Beidou, Chevreuse, or Sandrone)
```

- Sundays: Every domain is open. Use this day exclusively to smooth out whichever material stack is lagging behind.
