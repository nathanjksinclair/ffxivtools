## What is involved

Each submarine has stats. Generally speaking there is a Surv stat which is most critical, but you will also want to increase other stats
to maximise how many items you can get through each run. If just getting the item is the only thing of importance to you, then increase the Surv
stat.

The table of items for getting rare items can be found [here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1eiuGRrQTjJ5n4ogybKOBxJRT7EiJLlX93fge-Z8MmD4/edit?gid=548933443#gid=548933443)
